What is Defend Your Experience?

Defend Your Experience is an AI interview-defense coach. It is designed to help students confidently explain and defend the experience they put on their resumes, portfolios and project descriptions.

Dashboard

Add your real experience and begin extracting claims.

My Claims

Review the meaningful statements that an interviewer could challenge.

Defense Interview

Practice one question at a time. The next question should become deeper based on your previous answer.

Defense Report

Identify strong claims, weak claims and areas where you need better evidence.

Session History

Review your previous practice sessions.

Important principle

Defend, don't decorate. The system should use your real experience as its source of truth and should never invent achievements or evidence.
