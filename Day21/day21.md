# Day 21 – Digital Privacy Dashboard

## Objective
Built and tested an AI-generated Digital Privacy Dashboard using Claude.

## Features
- Digital Footprint Score
- Privacy Score
- Exposure Heatmap
- Company Exposure Ranking
- Data Collection Matrix
- Risk Radar
- Digital Twin Profile
- Most Valuable Data Assets
- Privacy Improvement Simulator
- Final Verdict

## Testing
- Opened the HTML dashboard in the browser.
- Verified all dashboard sections.
- Tested the Privacy Improvement Simulator.
- Reviewed charts, rankings, and interactive elements.

## Privacy Findings
- Digital Footprint Score: 74/100
- Privacy Score: 41/100
- Highest exposure areas:
  - Google ecosystem
  - Social media
  - Digital payments
- Privacy simulator demonstrated how privacy settings can improve the score.

## Learnings
- Learned how AI can generate complex interactive dashboards.
- Understood how digital footprints can be visualized.
- Learned about privacy risks across different online services.
- Practiced testing AI-generated HTML applications.
