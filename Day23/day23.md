Day 23 — Customer & MVP Blueprint

Startup

AI Placement Coach for Indian College Students

Executive Summary

An AI-powered placement coach for Indian engineering/CS undergraduates (years 2–4), using an Assess → Diagnose → Plan → Practice → Review → Simulate → Measure workflow.

The report identifies the wedge as prioritization, personalization, and accountability, rather than more content. Its verdict is conditional GO: build a narrow assessment + roadmap MVP and validate activation, retention, and willingness to pay.

Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)

Age/stage: 18–24, primarily university years 2–4

Market: India; engineering & technology undergraduates

Goal: software engineering internship or full-time placement

Current behavior: uses LeetCode, GeeksforGeeks, YouTube and generic AI tools but wants structure

Core pain: doesn't know what to prioritize or whether preparation is sufficient

Willingness to pay: conditional on clear personalization and outcomes

Reach: campus communities, coding clubs, student creators, referrals

Buyer Persona

The Placement-Focused Student — regularly studies DSA and tutorials, has tried multiple free resources, but remains uncertain whether effort is focused on the right topics. Placement season creates anxiety about falling behind peers.

Core objection: “Why should I pay when YouTube, GeeksforGeeks, LeetCode and free AI tools already exist?”

Top 10 Customer Pain Points

Don't know what to study next — High — AI personalized roadmap

Too many scattered resources — High — Single curated daily plan

Inconsistent practice/no accountability — High — Goal tracking + streaks/reminders

Can't judge placement readiness — High — Placement Readiness Score

Weak areas remain hidden — Medium/High — Diagnostic assessment

Interview anxiety — High — Mock interview simulator (post-MVP)

Project portfolio uncertainty — Medium — Role-aligned project recommendations

Generic AI answers don't persist/adapt — Medium — Persistent curriculum + progress memory

Low confidence entering assessments — Medium — Weekly progress feedback

Unclear companies/roles to target — Medium — Role/company-specific prep paths

Customer Journey

Awareness: campus community, student creator, or placement-readiness search.

Consideration: takes the free readiness assessment and compares it with existing free routines.

Purchase: converts after seeing a personalized roadmap and an early win in the first 3–7 days.

Retention: weekly reassessment, daily task streaks, later mock interviews, and placement outcomes encourage return and referrals.

Buying Triggers

Placement season/internship application opens

Failed or upcoming online coding assessment

Feeling behind peers

Need for a defined 30/60/90-day plan

Customer Objections

Free alternatives already exist

Low student budgets/price sensitivity

Skepticism about AI readiness-score accuracy

Reluctance to adopt another platform

MVP Recommendation

Build first

Free diagnostic assessment → AI-generated personalized roadmap → daily task list → basic progress tracking

Do NOT build yet

Mock interview simulator, project recommendation engine, company-specific tracks, native mobile app, gamification/social features, or an original content library.

Success metrics

Assessment completion, Day-7 activation/return, daily task completion, monetization conversion, and qualitative feedback from 20–50 test users.

MoSCoW Prioritization

Must Have: diagnostic assessment, AI roadmap generator, daily task assignment, basic progress tracking.

Should Have: Placement Readiness Score, weekly reassessment, email/WhatsApp reminders.

Could Have: mock interview simulator, project recommendations, peer/percentile benchmarking.

Won't Have (MVP): full content library, native mobile app, company-specific question banks, gamification/social feed, counseling marketplace.

Pricing Hypothesis

Freemium entry: free diagnostic assessment + first roadmap.

Paid tier: daily tracking, readiness-score updates and later mock interviews.

Illustrative prices to test:

₹199–₹299/month

₹999–₹1,999/year

These are planning assumptions, not validated prices.

Top 5 Risks

Crowded market — High/High — own the narrow readiness workflow.

Unproven willingness to pay — High/High — run a real payment test before further build.

Weak retention — High/High — anchor around daily workflow and visible progress.

AI commoditization — High/Medium-High — build persistent progress history and workflow value.

Customer acquisition cost — Medium-High/Medium-High — use campus communities, creators and referrals.

30-Day MVP Plan

Days 1–5: 20–30 student interviews

Days 6–10: landing page + free assessment; target 50–100 signups

Days 11–17: MVP build

Days 18–24: test with 20–50 real users

Days 25–27: test ₹199–₹299/month or ₹999–₹1,999/year

Days 28–30: double down / pivot / stop

Founder Action Sheet

Conduct 20–30 structured student interviews.

Ship a landing page with the free readiness assessment.

Build a 10–15-question diagnostic.

Build v1 of the AI roadmap generator.

Drive 50–100 signups through student communities.

Track activation and Day-7 retention.

Test with 20–50 real students.

Test pricing before Day 28.

Define kill/pivot triggers.

Make the Day 28–30 decision decisively.

Final Conclusion

PROMISING BUT UNVALIDATED

The report says the problem thesis and addressable market are strong, but there are currently no paying customers, LOIs, or waitlist and differentiation is unproven. The correct next step is the narrow 30-day MVP test with explicit activation, retention, and payment-based kill/pivot criteria.

Key Learnings

Focus on prioritization and accountability, not another content library.

Start with the diagnostic assessment + personalized roadmap.

Measure retention and willingness to pay before adding features.

Curate existing resources rather than recreating them.

Validate customer pain through interviews before assuming it.

Treat pricing as a hypothesis until real customers are tested.
