What is Defend Your Experience?

It is an interview-defense coach that helps you identify statements about your real experience and later challenge those statements with specific questions about ownership, evidence, decisions, outcomes, trade-offs and lessons.

Day 4 collects and organizes those claims. It does not rewrite your resume.
