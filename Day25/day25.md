## Startup Summary

PlacePrep AI is an AI-powered placement coach for Indian engineering and computer science students. It assesses students' skills, identifies weak areas, creates a personalized preparation roadmap, assigns daily tasks, and tracks placement readiness.

## Key Features

- Diagnostic assessment
- Personalized AI roadmap
- Daily preparation tasks
- Progress tracking
- Placement readiness evaluation

## Key Investor Learnings

1. The biggest execution risk is student retention and consistent usage.
2. Existing free resources make differentiation important.
3. Customer acquisition should initially focus on low-cost campus communities, coding clubs, creators, referrals, and organic content.
4. CAC and LTV are currently hypotheses and need real-user validation.
5. The MVP should be validated with 20–50 real students before scaling.
6. Pricing should be tested rather than assumed.
7. A feature alone is not a strong moat; execution, distribution, data, and user outcomes are more important.

## My Learning

The simulation helped me understand how investors evaluate a startup beyond the idea itself. I learned that market size, execution, customer retention, unit economics, profitability, competition, and scalability are all important when pitching a startup.
