# Day 24 — Business Strategy Validation

## Startup
**AI Placement Coach for Indian College Students**

## Objective
Evaluate whether the startup can become a sustainable business using the Day 23 Customer & MVP Blueprint, with emphasis on revenue, distribution, evidence, and practical execution.

## Resources Reviewed
- Day 23 Customer & MVP Blueprint
- Business Strategy Report
- Visual Dashboard

## Key Business Learnings
1. **The real wedge is workflow, not content.** Students already have LeetCode, GeeksforGeeks, YouTube and generic AI tools. The product must win by deciding what the student should do next and keeping them accountable.
2. **The customer is clear, but evidence is not.** The target is mainly Indian engineering/CS students in years 2–4 preparing for internships or placements. Direct interviews and payment evidence are still required.
3. **The MVP should stay narrow.** Diagnostic assessment → personalized roadmap → daily tasks → basic progress tracking is enough to test the core hypothesis.
4. **Revenue depends on retention.** At the proposed student price point, recurring usage and low acquisition cost matter more than downloads or signups.
5. **Freemium is a funnel, not the business model.** The free assessment must create enough early value that some users convert to a paid plan.
6. **Community-led distribution is the best initial GTM.** Campus groups, coding clubs, student creators and referrals fit the ICP better than paid ads during validation.
7. **The moat must be earned.** The LLM itself is not defensible. Progress history, readiness calibration, outcome data and distribution partnerships can become stronger over time.
8. **Verdict: VALIDATE.** The startup is promising but unvalidated. The next milestone is activation + Day-7 retention + payment + repeatable acquisition, not feature expansion.

## Business Reality Check
- Who pays: placement-focused students.
- Why pay: personalized prioritization, accountability and measurable readiness.
- Discovery: campus communities, coding clubs, creators, referrals and high-intent search.
- Growth risks: fragmented distribution and rising CAC.
- Monetization risks: free substitutes, generic AI and student price sensitivity.
- Weak assumptions: willingness to pay, retention, trust in readiness scoring and differentiation.

## Revenue Strategy
Illustrative pricing from the blueprint:
- Monthly: ₹199–₹299/month
- Annual: ₹999–₹1,999/year

These prices remain hypotheses until a real checkout/payment test validates them.

## Go-To-Market
**Campus / creators / search → free readiness assessment → personalized roadmap → 3–7 day early win → paid subscription → weekly progress → referral.**

## First 100 Users
- 10–15 research users
- 20–25 pilot users
- 25–30 campus/community users
- 20–25 creator/referral users

## Competitive Position & Moat
Do not compete with LeetCode, GfG, HackerRank or YouTube on content volume. Become the orchestration layer over resources students already use.

Potential moat progression:
**Workflow quality → progress history → readiness calibration → outcome dataset + distribution.**

## Founder Action Plan
1. Conduct 20–30 structured interviews.
2. Launch the assessment landing page.
3. Build the 10–15 question diagnostic.
4. Ship the prompt-based roadmap generator.
5. Recruit the first 50–100 users.
6. Instrument activation and Day-7 retention.
7. Run a 20–50 student pilot.
8. Test real payment at the proposed price range.
9. Define kill/pivot triggers.
10. Make the Day 28–30 double-down/pivot/stop decision.

## Final Verdict
🟡 **VALIDATE** — The business has a credible problem and sensible MVP, but sustainability is not yet proven. The next step is evidence, not feature expansion.
