# AI Placement Coach - Business Strategy Report

## Executive Summary
AI-powered placement coach for Indian engineering and CS students in years 2-4. Core wedge: prioritization, personalization and accountability rather than more content. The business is promising but unvalidated. Verdict: VALIDATE.

## Business Reality Check
- Who pays: placement-focused students.
- Why pay: personalized prioritization, accountability and measurable readiness.
- Discovery: campus communities, coding clubs, student creators, referrals and high-intent search.
- Growth risks: fragmented distribution and rising CAC.
- Monetization risks: free substitutes, generic AI and price sensitivity.
- Weak assumptions: willingness to pay, retention, trust in readiness scoring and differentiation.

## Business Model Canvas
| Block | Strategy |
|---|---|
| Customer | Indian engineering and CS students, years 2-4 |
| Value | Personalized preparation workflow and readiness feedback |
| Channels | Campus, clubs, creators, referrals, search |
| Relationship | Daily tasks, reminders, progress feedback |
| Revenue | Freemium plus monthly and annual subscription |
| Resources | Assessment logic, roadmap prompts, progress history |
| Partners | Campus communities, clubs, creators |
| Costs | Product/AI, acquisition, support, development |

## Revenue and Pricing
Free diagnostic plus first roadmap. Test INR 199-299/month and INR 999-1,999/year. These are hypotheses, not validated prices. Key metrics: free-to-paid conversion, Day-7 retention, plan adherence, churn and CAC.

## Go-To-Market Strategy
Campus / creators / search -> free assessment -> personalized roadmap -> 3-7 day early win -> paid subscription -> weekly progress -> referral. Community-led distribution should lead validation; paid ads should wait until funnel economics are proven.

## Customer Acquisition Strategy
Founder-led interviews -> pilot cohort -> coding-club partnerships -> student creator referrals -> high-intent assessment landing page -> referrals. Test paid ads only after organic and community conversion is proven.

## First 100 Users Plan
- 10-15 research users
- 20-25 pilot users
- 25-30 campus/community users
- 20-25 creator/referral users

## Competitive Position and Moat
Do not compete with LeetCode, GfG, HackerRank or YouTube on content volume. Become the orchestration layer over resources students already use.

Moat progression: workflow quality -> progress history -> readiness calibration -> outcome data plus distribution. The LLM itself is not defensible.

## Reverse SWOT
Strength: clear pain thesis, narrow MVP, large reachable market.

Weakness: no direct interviews, paying customers or strong differentiation evidence.

Opportunity: placement urgency and community distribution.

Threat: free tools and generic AI can imitate features.

## Investor One-Liner
An AI placement coach that turns fragmented DSA and interview resources into a personalized daily preparation system with progress-based readiness feedback for Indian engineering students.

## 30-Second Founder Pitch
Indian engineering students have more placement content than they can consume, yet many still do not know what to study next or whether they are ready. We are building an AI placement coach that diagnoses gaps, creates a personalized plan, assigns daily tasks and measures progress using resources students already trust. The proof point is not downloads - it is whether students return, execute the plan and pay.

## Sustainability Verdict
VALIDATE. The startup has a credible recurring problem and sensible MVP, but sustainability is not proven. The next milestone is activation, Day-7 retention, payment and repeatable acquisition - not feature expansion.
