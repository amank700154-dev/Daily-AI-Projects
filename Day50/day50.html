What is Defend Your Experience?

This application helps you defend claims about your real experience. It does not rewrite your resume. It extracts meaningful claims, challenges them like a skeptical interviewer, follows up based on your answers, and reports where your evidence is strong or weak.

My Claims = what you must defend. Defense Interview = adaptive questioning. Defense Report = strengths and gaps. Your own evidence remains the source of truth.
