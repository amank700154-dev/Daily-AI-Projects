Important methodology note

The application does not use fabricated product facts. Product fields are sourced manufacturer specifications or clearly identified manufacturer claims. The live fit score is a derived calculation from those factual fields and the user's weights. It is not a manufacturer benchmark or a claimed product score.

Battery claims are not perfectly apples-to-apples because manufacturers can use different test conditions. The UI therefore labels battery values as claims rather than presenting them as a universal lab benchmark.

The Dell XPS 13 India product page currently indicates that the product is unavailable; the application surfaces that fact instead of guessing stock or price.
