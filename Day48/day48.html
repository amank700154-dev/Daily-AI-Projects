How this was researched

Each option was checked against manufacturer product/specification pages. Where a manufacturer supplied a claimed battery runtime, that claim is shown as-is and not silently converted into a measured benchmark. The Dell XPS 13 page currently marks the product unavailable in India; that availability status is therefore surfaced rather than inferred.

  • Price: India manufacturer/store listing where available.
  • Weight: manufacturer technical specifications.
  • Battery: manufacturer stated runtime when available; otherwise battery capacity is retained as the factual field.
  • Display: manufacturer resolution/brightness/refresh specifications.
  • Conflicts: battery claims are not directly comparable across brands because test methods differ. The app labels the ranking as a derived decision aid rather than a lab benchmark.
No synthetic product facts are used. Derived ranking values are calculated from the cited factual fields and the user's selected weights.
